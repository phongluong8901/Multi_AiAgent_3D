Phien ban: 1.0.0

Kien truc: Windows x64 (Windows 10 / Windows 11)

[1] TONG QUAN
Duck AI Manager la tro ly de ban thong minh (Desktop Mate) ho tro theo doi
he thong, quan ly tien trinh, phan tich ung dung dang mo, chuoi hanh dong da lenh
(Multi-Tool Chaining), am thanh vat ly da huong (Spatial Sound FX), va tuong tac
3D truc quan tren man hinh.

[2] CACH CHAY UNG DUNG
- Nhan dup chuot vao tep: "Duck ai Manager.exe" de khoi dong.
- Luu y: Giu nguyen tep "WebView2Loader.dll" cung thu muc voi "Duck ai Manager.exe".

[3] YEU CAU HE THONG
- He dieu hanh: Windows 10 (version 1903 tro len) hoac Windows 11 64-bit.
- Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (da duoc tich hop san tren hau het Windows 10/11).
  Neu may chu chua co, tai tai: https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/webview2/

[4] CAC TINH NANG NOI BAT
- Nhan dien va tuong tac vat ly cua so Windows (bouncing, landing, dropping).
- Theo doi thong so CPU, RAM, GPU, Disk, Network theo thoi gian thuc.
- Chuoi hanh dong da lenh: phan tich, dieu huong, tu dong hoa theo kich ban.
- He thong am thanh vat ly Spatial Audio FX voi Web Audio API.
- Giao dien toi uu 5.1MB sieu nhe, khong can cai dat phuc tap.
